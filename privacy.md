# Privacy policy – Brzi Parking

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Brzi Parking ("we") is a free app for paying for parking by SMS in Novi Sad, Serbia. Contact: [brziparking@gmail.com](mailto:brziparking@gmail.com).

## In short

- No account, sign-up or login.
- Your plates, parking history and location stay **only on your phone**. We don't receive, store or sell them.
- The app has no ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
- Your plate leaves the phone only in the SMS you send to pay for parking.

## What the app keeps on your phone

This data is kept in the app's local database and is not sent to us or anyone else:

| Data                                                                                                            | Why                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Your vehicles' licence plates and names                                                                         | So the payment SMS contains the right plate                             |
| Parking history: plate, zone, SMS number, message text, time, and, if location was on, the place where you paid | Countdown, reminders, extending and the list of active parkings         |
| Remembered places: location, zone and time of each payment or manual zone choice                                | So the same zone is suggested straight away the next time you are there |
| Settings (language, theme, reminders)                                                                           | So the app works the way you want                                       |

**Phone backup:** if automatic backup is on (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone), the system may include this app's data. That backup is kept by Google or Apple under their own rules and is tied to your account; we have no access to it.

## Location

- The app uses your location **only while it is open**, to find the zone you are in. There is no background tracking.
- Location is processed on the phone and is not sent to us.
- You can refuse or revoke the permission in your phone's settings. You then pick the zone by hand.

## The payment SMS

When you pay, the app opens your messaging app with an SMS ready to send to the zone's number (for example 8212), and the message text is your licence plate. You send the message from your own number. It is then handled by your mobile operator and JKP "Parking servis" Novi Sad under their own privacy rules. We never see it.

## Map

To show the map, the app downloads map tiles from the internet:

- **Android:** from [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/privacy/), which uses Cloudflare. The server sees your IP address and which part of the city is shown, but not your exact location. According to its policy, OpenFreeMap keeps server logs for at most 7 days (up to 30 days for security incidents) and uses no cookies.
- **iPhone:** Apple Maps, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Without internet the map doesn't load; everything else still works.

## Reminders

Reminders before your parking ends are scheduled by the phone itself. There is no notification server and nothing is sent off the phone.

## What we don't do

We have no server holding your data. We use no ads, analytics, tracking tools or crash reporting. We don't sell or share data, because we don't have any.

## Deleting your data

- In the app: **Settings → Delete all data** removes vehicles, history, remembered places and settings.
- Uninstalling the app removes all of its data (except a phone backup, if you have one; delete that in your Google or Apple account settings).

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data about them.

## Your rights

Since we don't collect your personal data, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete: it is all on your phone and under your control. For data from SMS payments, contact your mobile operator or JKP "Parking servis". If you believe your rights have been violated, you can complain to Serbia's [Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection](https://www.poverenik.rs).

## Changes

If the app gains anything that changes this policy (for example downloading newer zone data from the internet), we will update this text and the date at the top first.
